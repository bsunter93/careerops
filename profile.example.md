# Candidate profile

This file is fed verbatim into the fit-scoring prompt. Its quality sets the ceiling on
score quality: a vague profile produces confident nonsense. Write it as if briefing a
recruiter who will be asked follow-up questions.

`profile.md` is gitignored. Copy this to `profile.md` and replace everything.

## Summary

Two or three sentences. What you do, at what scale, in what domains.

## Targeting

- Track: IC or management. State it plainly; it is scored.
- Levels: the titles and bands you are actually aiming at.
- Functions: the job families you want, and the ones you do not.

## Experience

For each role: company, title, dates, and the three or four claims that would survive a
follow-up question. Prefer "1.56 to 0.46 FTE per 100 cases" over "cut overhead 70%".

## Skills

Tools and methods you would be comfortable being tested on.

## Preferences and dealbreakers

The section that does the most work. Anything listed as a dealbreaker caps the score
below 40 regardless of skill match, so be honest rather than aspirational.

- Compensation floor.
- Location and remote constraints.
- Dealbreakers, e.g. "role's core mandate is building and managing a team".

## Known gaps

Say them out loud. The scorer uses them to avoid inventing experience you do not have,
which is what produces scores you cannot trust.
